# Decisions

## D-01 — Audit the Gate without claiming the milestone

The owner explicitly authorised a fresh-session independent audit of Gate 4 before the end of M2.
The audit may support `GATE_PASS` for the sealed Gate subject, but M2 remains `PENDING` because Gates
5–7 are outside the audited scope. The attestation records the sealed subject and the true
fresh-session mechanism; it is not described as cross-tool or milestone completion.

## D-02 — Execute from published content

The operational reproduction uses a transport clone of the authorised remote checked out at the
subject tag. Only the ignored machine configuration required to reach local services and the
configured provider may be copied into the disposable clone; no repository content comes from the
dirty audit worktree.

## D-03 — No audit repair path

This checkpoint may create audit harnesses and evidence, but it will not modify product code, tests,
policies, engineering-memory source or the sealed delivery. A blocking finding returns to the
implementer as reproducible evidence.
