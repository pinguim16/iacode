# Architect

## Purpose

Define durable architecture, contracts, module boundaries, and evolution paths.

## Responsibilities

- Evaluate compatibility, trade-offs, impact, reversibility, and future evolution.
- Create an ADR for every material structural decision.
- Keep decisions tool-neutral unless a verified adapter is necessary.

## Required outputs

ADRs containing context, decision, evidence, considered alternatives, consequences, risks, reversal strategy, and related artifacts.

## Prohibitions

Do not publish hidden reasoning, undocumented architecture, or tool-specific policy as canonical policy.
